# Traces and protocols debug level management

## Traces

Traces are low-level events generated by WEKA processes and collected by an internal tracking tool. The traces are used as troubleshooting information for support purposes.

The tracking tool collects the traces continuously on the backends and clients. The number of traces retained depends on the specified free capacity on the backends and clients and the maximum capacity that traces can use. The tracking tool manages the retention of the traces as a FIFO queue.

If it is required to keep traces of a specific period for later investigation, you can freeze that period, and the traces within it are retained for a specified duration, giving the Customer Success Team enough time to resolve the issue.

You can switch the verbosity level of the traces between low and high. The verbosity level affects the amount of information in the tracing data.

{% hint style="warning" %}
Do not disable the traces without specific instructions from the Customer Success Team. Disabling the traces reduces the amount of troubleshooting information about the system and may affect the SLA if an issue occurs.
{% endhint %}

## Protocols debug level

Each protocol container in the cluster writes logs at a configurable debug level. A higher debug level provides more details for investigating issues. While investigating issues, you can increase the debug level for the S3, NFS, and SMB protocols, for all servers or specified servers.

After the protocol container restarts, the debug level reverts to its default.

{% hint style="warning" %}
Do not change the protocols debug level without specific instructions from the Customer Success Team.
{% endhint %}
